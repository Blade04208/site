BackBlade0 Visual Design

My own brand design has a very complicated and silly history. I consider the "start" of my brand to be in 2023, when I found myself looking through the standard list of Microsft Fluent Icons and found a set of animal icons: Dog, Cat, Turtle and Rabbit. However, only the rabbit has a disabled variant, which quickly became an in-joke among friends. This "in-joke" evolved several times, starting when doromiert asked me to use a profile picture invoving the icon.

Initial profile picture

This initial variant of the design language used Inter and the unchanged Microsoft icon.
Later, in 2024, I remade the icon from scratch to avoid any copyright issues, along with changing the background to a blue variant of NUDL 8's Jump wallpaper. The primary font used changed to Atkinson Hyperlegible. In 2025, blade0.net was launched with this design system.

Blade0.net's first iteration

In 2026, Jump and blade0.net was modified to have a more glassmorphic look, up until July, where the design is what you see today. My design language focuses on simplicity and readability, while also allowing for cool experiments.
